# Historical Research Findings

Research only. No live trading results or proven future advantage.

All results below are historical BTC-USD simulations with
$1,000 starting cash and a $150 position budget.

Each calendar year was tested independently.
Annual profits must not be interpreted as compounded returns.

Trading costs: 1.2% fee and 0.05% adverse slippage per side.

| Strategy | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Momentum | -19.49 | +72.60 | +32.39 | -19.03 |
| Original Overton | -95.16 | 0.00 | +22.66 | -11.62 |
| Overton recovery 0.25% | -93.71 | 0.00 | +1.97 | -13.68 |
| Simplified TrendRider | -256.56 | -336.81 | -292.07 | -304.45 |

Amounts are net simulated USD profit or loss.

## Findings

- Momentum had positive results in two of four years,
  but underperformed its buy-and-hold benchmark overall.
- Original Overton was inconsistent across market conditions.
- The 0.25% recovery rule did not consistently improve Overton.
- Simplified TrendRider lost money in all four years.

## Limitations

- Historical prices and simulated executions only.
- Several strategy ideas were investigated repeatedly.
- Tests are not pristine independent holdouts.
- Intrabar execution assumptions vary between programs.
- TrendRider is a simplified approximation, not the original.
- These results do not demonstrate future profitability.

All archived programs are experimental and require further
dependency, methodology, and security review before publication.
